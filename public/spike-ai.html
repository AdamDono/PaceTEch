How does the 5-10% daily ROI target work? +
SpikeBot targets a 5-10% gain on the account's opening balance per session. This is achieved by catching the "post-spike" stability on BOOM/CRASH indices. The Anti-Loss brain kills trades instantly if drawdown limits are hit, ensuring your principal capital remains safe even on volatile days.
What is the buy-back and burn mechanic? +
20% of each profitable trading session's profits are used to purchase $SPIKE tokens from the open market. These tokens are immediately sent to a dead address (0x000...dead) and permanently removed from the 100M supply. Every burn transaction is verifiable on Base.
Why Base network? +
Base offers near-zero gas fees, Ethereum-level security, and fast transaction finality — making micro buy-back transactions economically viable. It also benefits from Coinbase's infrastructure and growing DEX liquidity.
What is the difference between private and public presale rounds? +
The private round (23M tokens at $0.0008) is invite-only and subject to a 6-month linear vesting term. The public presale (40M tokens at $0.001) is open to all and fully liquid at launch. Both are at a steep discount to the $0.005 launch price.
